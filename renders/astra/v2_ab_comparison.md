Godwyn X-slash v2 — original character versus upgraded character

The approved animation was copied from `models/astra_xslash_v2_wip.blend` into `models/astra_xslash_v2_on_char_wip.blend`. No motion was re-authored. The comparison uses the original v2 lighting, world, exposure (-0.7), cameras, frame numbers and 0.55-frame shutter. In every `v2_on_char/ab_*.png` pair, the original is on the left and the upgraded character is on the right.

**Rig binding:** clean, with no retargeting. All 121 bone names, parents and rest matrices match. The copied action retains all 847 curves and 302,379 keys, including interpolation and handles. Comparing every bone at all 357 quarter-frame samples produced zero matrix difference. The standalone windup/cross/follow snapshots also reproduce exactly on a fresh upgraded-character input. See `v2_on_char/transfer_verification.json` and `pose_portability_verification.json`.

**Secondary motion:** all 97 original secondary bones survive and animate identically: 21 cape, 64 robe and 12 hair bones. The existing three-frame root lag and two-frame-per-child delay remain in the copied keys. There are no separately named sleeve lag bones in the source; the new inner sleeves use the arm/forearm binding. Consequently, identical bone motion does not give identical fabric silhouettes. The raised sleeve no longer pulls the large hanging triangular sheet seen in the old F27/F36 images. The cape hem still trails the body and settles. Short jagged gold strips and a comb-like fringe remain visible around the side waist/skirt, especially in three-quarter F27/F36/F40, and the shoulder/underarm transition still looks reconstructed.

**Sword through blur:** mixed. The new cool steel edge and gold hilt separate more clearly in the held and recovery poses; front F40 also has a clearer silver blade against the gold chest. Front F46 becomes very dark against the background; F57 is also worse: the blade is more foreshortened and largely disappears into the hand/chest blur. In three-quarter F57 the pale fan is visible, but its exact blade silhouette remains indistinct. This is not a changed wrist animation: the upgraded sword retains its different native rigid RightHand attachment and shorter blade (about 1.47 m versus 1.65 m grip-to-tip). The 14 existing temporal ribbons were resampled along that actual blade with their original timing, opacity and material. No attachment correction or weapon retargeting was introduced.

**Materials/shading:** under identical lighting, the new gold is stronger yellow and more mirror-like, with conspicuous bright highlights and dark patches. It can dominate the chest and arm silhouette. The robe changes from bright royal blue to darker, greyer indigo; it looks more textured and subdued, but some fold detail is harder to read. Gold streaking/patchiness and the ragged trim still interrupt broad cloth areas. The new face and hair appear paler and flatter at this framing. The steel sword has clearer material separation. These are observations of the supplied character; its geometry, weights, textures and material graphs were not edited.
