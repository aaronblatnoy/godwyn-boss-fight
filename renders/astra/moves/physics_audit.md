# Godwyn move-set physics audit

Each section is appended only after its move has been rendered, visually reviewed and encoded. Source: a read-only copy of `models/astra_character_v2_prechar2.blend` retained as `models/astra_move_character_base.blend` so concurrent character-track edits cannot change the base between moves. No source character/X-slash/cinematography files are modified.

## Method and limitations

Same kinematic method as `renders/astra/naturalness_audit.md`: 121 bones sampled at quarter-frame intervals, shortest quaternion-log angular velocity (antipodal signs are equivalent), integer-frame angular acceleration and reversal/spike candidates. Candidate thresholds: reversal dot < −0.5 with both steps > 0.5°; speed > max(4°/frame, 2.8× local seven-sample median); acceleration > 12°/frame². These are screening thresholds, not torque limits.

Anatomy uses actual joint heads rather than the imported display tails. Elbow/knee flexion is zero at straight; wrist screening compares forearm and hand longitudinal axes. Broad screening thresholds are 150° elbow flexion and 80° wrist bend. Shoulder elevation, segment-length errors, asymmetric left/right speed correlations, blade tip speed and floor clearance are retained in per-move metrics. This is not a calibrated anatomical or muscle/torque simulation. Sword-mass sensitivity additionally tests 2%, 4% and 6% of body mass, with sword COM 45% from hilt to tip; results are in `<move>_sword_mass_sensitivity.json`.

COM uses torso 49.7%, head 8.1%, and each thigh 10%, shank 4.65%, foot 1.45%, upper arm 2.8%, forearm 1.6%, hand 0.6%. Segment centers and support hull match the prior audit (20 cm boot width, 12 cm heel extension, 9 cm toe extension, 2 cm inset). Grounded support requires an intended planted foot and measured sole clearance below 15 mm. Static COM outside that hull is disclosed; a caught step or flight is identified explicitly, not labelled statically stable.

Sole and hem clearance is measured on the evaluated skinned body mesh at every integer frame against Z = −0.015 m. Foot targets are corrected from measured sole height; distal cloth locations gather cloth upward while waist and upper chain roots remain attached. Corrections, before/after values and all angular flags remain in `<move>_contacts.json` and `<move>_metrics.json`. No hidden cloth/body triangle collision certification is claimed. Cloth is a deterministic delayed/damped kinematic response, not a fabric simulation.

Loop clips bake the duplicate endpoint in the blend but omit it from the MP4 to avoid a held seam frame. World pose equality is checked modulo any documented root displacement; quarter-frame finite-difference seam velocities are reported. Render: Blender 5.2.1, Metal, BLENDER_EEVEE, 768×768, 30 fps. The stable pre-char2 character is used without the later face/hair track or its material workarounds. Contact sheets contain labelled frames, with every second active frame for attacks. MP4s are libx264/yuv420p and fully decoded for validation.
